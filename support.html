📖 About the App

Live Results is an informational app that displays publicly available lottery and game results in real time. The app does not facilitate any form of betting, wagering, or purchasing of tickets. It is intended solely for users who wish to check results conveniently on their iPhone.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What does this app do?

Live Results shows publicly available lottery draw results in real time. It is a read-only information display app — no betting, no purchases, and no account required.

Why are results not showing or not updating?

Please check your internet connection. Pull down on the results screen to refresh. If results still do not load, close the app fully and reopen it.

How often are results updated?

Results are updated automatically as soon as they are officially published. The app fetches the latest data each time you open or refresh the screen.

Does the app require an account or personal information?

No. The app does not require registration, login, or any personal information to use.

The app says an update is available. What should I do?

Open the App Store, search for Live Results, and tap Update. Keeping the app updated ensures you see the latest results correctly.

The screen is blank or showing an error.

This is usually a temporary network issue. Close the app, check your internet connection, and reopen. If the problem persists, please contact us.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Live Results does not collect, store, or share any personal data. No account is required and no user information is transmitted to our servers. The app only fetches publicly available result data to display on your device. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
